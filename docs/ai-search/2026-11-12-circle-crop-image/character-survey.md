### C-S4: C-Q4

- Visible search queries / process summary: Not shown (no web search)
- Recommended brands in order: Canva
- Brands only mentioned: Canva Pro
- Reasons given (verbatim): "Works on iPhone, Android and computer."
- Sources: none
- Full response:

> To crop your picture into a circle without making it blurry, the trick is to keep the original image quality and export it at a high resolution.
> Easiest way: Use Canva — Works on iPhone, Android and computer.
> Open Canva and create a square design (ideally 1080 × 1080 pixels or larger). / Go to Elements → Frames and choose the circle frame. / Upload your original photo and drag it into the circle. / Double-tap the photo to adjust its position and size. / Tap Share → Download. / Choose PNG for the best quality, especially if you want a transparent background.
> How to prevent blurriness: Use the original photo, not a screenshot or a photo saved from social media. / Don't zoom in too much when positioning the picture inside the circle. / Keep the resolution high — aim for at least 1080 × 1080 pixels. / Save as PNG, rather than repeatedly saving as JPEG.
> If you need just the circle with no square background, select Transparent background when exporting (this may require Canva Pro).
> Quick question: Are you cropping the picture on an iPhone, Android or computer? I can show you the easiest way for your device, including how to keep the circle crystal clear.
