# Ordalin logo research

Research date: 2026-08-22

## Working brief

Ordalin is an inventory-first AI tools directory for global-English users. Its identity should make ordered discovery feel trustworthy, clear, perceptive, and editorial. It should feel durable rather than tied to short-lived AI visual conventions.

Primary applications are the website navigation and footer, 16/24/32 px favicon, source mark on tool cards, social avatar/share image, and light/dark themes. The final system must support both a compact symbol and a full wordmark.

Avoided conventions: AI sparkles, robots, neural networks, magic wands, magnifying glasses, literal toolboxes or directory lists, an initial inside a circle, sci-fi styling, growth-hacker gradients, and direct imitation of Product Hunt or SaaSHub.

## Originality screen

Image searches were run for notched-O wordmarks, paired open frames, negative-space passages, and continuous OD monograms. Generic notched circles and symmetrical overlapping squares were heavily represented, so those constructions were rejected. The four retained concepts use different semantic sources and signature moves:

1. **Editorial Cut** — name-led; a custom O is opened by an angled editorial cut rather than enclosed or decorated.
2. **Open Register** — product model; two offset registers form a diagonal route without becoming a literal list or box.
3. **Clear Passage** — user promise; two fields reveal a controlled route from noise to clarity.
4. **Ordinal Thread** — name and system behavior; one continuous line joins sequence, openness, and forward movement.

This is visual collision screening, not formal trademark clearance.
