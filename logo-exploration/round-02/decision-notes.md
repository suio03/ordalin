# Ordalin logo finalists — Round 02

## 01 Editorial Cut

The first-round heavy disc has been replaced by an open, calligraphic O. A precise diagonal insert makes the “edit” visible while leaving the mark breathable at small sizes. The O acts as the first letter of the full wordmark rather than sitting beside a repeated name.

- Best for: a durable, editorial brand with strong name ownership.
- Strongest trait: distinctive tone and recognisable silhouette.
- Remaining refinement after selection: custom letter spacing, outlined wordmark, and a simplified 16 px cut.

## 03 Clear Passage

The symmetrical S-shaped road has been removed. Two unequal editorial panels now create one controlled, angular passage, retaining the promise of clarity without resembling a literal road, map, or optical-illusion template.

- Best for: communicating the product promise quickly and clearly.
- Strongest trait: compact UI symbol with a direct discovery metaphor.
- Remaining refinement after selection: tune the inner gap at 16 px and decide whether the outer heights should be further exaggerated.

## Recommendation

Choose **01 Editorial Cut** if Ordalin should feel like a trusted editorial brand that can grow beyond an AI tools directory. Choose **03 Clear Passage** if immediate product comprehension matters more than a name-led personality.

Targeted image screening was repeated for open-O editorial marks and two-panel negative-space path marks. Literal road/pillar constructions were rejected. This is visual collision screening, not formal trademark clearance.
