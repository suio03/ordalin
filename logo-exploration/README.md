# Ordalin logo exploration — Round 01

Open `comparison.html` to review four equal, monochrome directions:

1. Editorial Cut — distinctive and editorial; strongest name ownership.
2. Open Register — structured and systematic; strongest favicon behavior.
3. Clear Passage — perceptive and human; clearest product promise.
4. Ordinal Thread — continuous and light; strongest motion potential.

Choose one direction, or at most two finalists. The selected direction will then be refined into a production symbol, full wordmark, responsive lockups, light/dark SVGs, favicon tests, and optional PNG exports.

These are exploratory assets. Typography is not outlined yet, and the originality screen is not formal trademark clearance.
