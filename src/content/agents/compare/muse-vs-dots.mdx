## Price

Muse starts at **$0**. The free tier has a usage limit Meta hasn't published. Two paid plans raise the weekly allowance: **Power ($20/mo, 500M Muse tokens a week)** and **Max ($100/mo, 3B Muse tokens a week)**. Meta says subscriptions are still in limited testing.

Dots has **no free tier**. It comes with **ChatGPT Pro ($100, $200 or $500 a month, monthly billing only)** or **Business Premium**. Your first dot is included at no extra cost, and the plan includes an allowance for deeper work, with extended limits in the first month. OpenAI says extra dots are coming "in the future" and hasn't announced a price. Pro $200 is open to new subscribers again; new subscriptions get a lower usage allowance than before.

**Bottom line:** if price decides it, Muse wins by at least $80 a month.

## What each can do

Meta describes Muse browsing the web, filling in forms, sending email, booking travel, making purchases and negotiating on your behalf. Its named payment and password partners are Link (by Stripe), Shop Pay and 1Password.

A dot has its own cloud computer and browser, works through connected apps (OpenAI says 4,000+ through plugins), runs scheduled and recurring tasks, and does proactive background research with read-only tools. You can also give it optional access to your laptop.

**Bottom line:** Muse is pitched at errands and purchases; Dots at ongoing work across the apps you already use.

## Where you use it

Muse runs on **iOS, Android and the web**, with glasses support "coming soon."

Dots lives inside ChatGPT: the **web and desktop apps** are where you set it up. The mobile app lets you chat with a dot after setup, but mobile web isn't supported. You can also message a dot from **Slack** and **Microsoft Teams**, and SMS is in a limited beta for US Pro users.

## Safety and privacy

Both do their work on a cloud machine rather than your phone. Muse works inside a **Muse Secure VM**, a dedicated cloud machine that holds the agent and your data, and a separate "Sentinel" agent must approve anything that reaches the internet. Meta has announced a **Confidential VM** with a key you hold, for later in 2026. It isn't live yet.

OpenAI **automatically reviews** actions that affect your accounts. You set **Custom Rules** for what a dot may do alone, and sensitive tasks like changing a password always stay with you. Resetting a dot deletes it along with its conversations, memories and scheduled tasks. Disconnecting an app doesn't delete data the dot already pulled from it.

**Bottom line:** Dots gives you more written-down controls today. Muse's strongest privacy feature, the Confidential VM, is announced but not shipped.

## Availability

Muse: **US, rolling out**. Dots: OpenAI's "eligible markets." We haven't recorded the country list yet. We'll add it once we've checked it against OpenAI's page.
