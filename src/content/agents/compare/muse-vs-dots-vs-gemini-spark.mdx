## Price

**Muse** starts at **$0**, with a usage limit Meta hasn't published. Power is **$20/mo** and Max **$100/mo**.

**Gemini Spark** has no plan of its own. It comes with **Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo)** or **Google AI Ultra ($99.99/mo, or $199.99/mo for the 20x tier)**. Google launched it as a beta for Ultra subscribers in the US before opening it to Pro.

**Dots** come with **ChatGPT Pro ($100, $200 or $500 a month)** or Business Premium. All three Pro tiers are open to new subscribers; only Pro $500 includes Astra Ultrafast.

**Bottom line:** Muse to start free; Gemini Spark for the cheapest paid plan; Dots if you already pay for ChatGPT Pro.

## What each can do

Meta says **Muse** browses the web, fills in forms, sends email, books travel, makes purchases and negotiates for you.

**Gemini Spark** runs multi-step tasks across Google's apps in the background. Google has added outside apps too: Canva, Dropbox, Instacart, OpenTable and Zillow Rentals, plus custom MCP connections. It can also run tasks on your Mac (beta, Ultra only).

A **dot** has its own cloud computer and browser, works through connected apps (OpenAI says 4,000+ through plugins), runs scheduled tasks and does background research.

**Bottom line:** Spark is the one to pick if your life runs on Google, Muse for errands, and Dots for work across many apps.

## Where you use it

- **Muse:** iOS, Android and the web.
- **Gemini Spark:** the Gemini app on the web and mobile; macOS beta for Ultra.
- **Dots:** set up on ChatGPT's web or desktop app; chat from the mobile app, Slack or Teams.

## Safety and privacy

**Muse** works in a dedicated cloud machine where a "Sentinel" agent must approve anything that reaches the internet; a Confidential VM with a key you hold is announced, not live.

**Gemini Spark** is designed to ask first before spending money or sending email, and its app connections are off by default.

**Dots** get automatic review of account-affecting actions and Custom Rules. On personal plans you choose whether dot conversations and work improve OpenAI's models.
