## Price

**Muse** starts at **$0**, with a usage limit Meta hasn't published. Power is **$20/mo** and Max **$100/mo**.

**Grok Bot** doesn't have a plan of its own. It comes with **SuperGrok ($30/mo)**, **SuperGrok Plus ($100/mo)** and SuperGrok Heavy (price not listed on SpaceXAI's pricing page), or with Cursor: **Cursor Pro ($20/mo)** and **Cursor Teams ($40 per user/mo)**. Its usage is counted separately and resets weekly.

**Dots** come with **ChatGPT Pro ($100, $200 or $500 a month)** or Business Premium. Your first dot is included. All three Pro tiers are open to new subscribers; only Pro $500 includes Astra Ultrafast.

**Bottom line:** Muse for free, Grok Bot for the cheapest paid plan, Dots if you're already on ChatGPT Pro.

## What each can do

Meta says **Muse** browses the web, fills in forms, sends email, books travel, makes purchases and negotiates for you.

A **dot** has its own cloud computer and browser, works through connected apps (OpenAI says 4,000+ through plugins), runs scheduled tasks and does background research with read-only tools.

Each **Grok Bot** gets a persistent cloud computer with a browser, a filesystem and a terminal. SpaceXAI says bots work across apps and websites, learn workflows from demonstration and coordinate with each other.

**Bottom line:** Muse is pitched at errands, Dots at ongoing work in your apps, and Grok Bot at multi-step jobs on a computer of its own.

## Where you use it

- **Muse:** iOS, Android and the web; glasses "coming soon."
- **Dots:** set up on ChatGPT's web or desktop app; chat from the mobile app, Slack or Microsoft Teams; SMS in a limited US beta.
- **Grok Bot:** macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android.

## Safety and privacy

**Muse** runs in a dedicated cloud machine, and a separate "Sentinel" agent must approve anything that reaches the internet. Its Confidential VM, with a key you hold, is announced for later in 2026 but not live.

**Dots** get automatic review of actions that affect your accounts, and you write Custom Rules for what a dot may do alone. Resetting a dot deletes its conversations, memories and scheduled tasks.

**Grok Bot** asks for approval before it sends messages, publishes, buys, transfers money, deletes data, changes permissions or accepts legal terms. You can add Auto Review rules; when an "ask first" rule and an "allow" rule both match, ask first wins. Deleting a bot doesn't remove files or sessions already on its cloud computer.

**Bottom line:** Grok Bot publishes the most specific approval list. Dots gives you the most rules to write. Muse's strongest privacy feature isn't shipped yet.
