## Price

**ChatGPT** has four steps above Free: **Go ($8/mo in the US**, localized elsewhere), **Plus ($20/mo)**, and **Pro at $100, $200 or $500 a month**. Pro $200 is open to new subscribers again, with a lower usage allowance than before; only Pro $500 includes Astra Ultrafast. Plus and Pro are billed monthly; there's no annual plan.

**Grok** has **Free**, **SuperGrok ($30/mo)** and **SuperGrok Plus ($100/mo)**. SpaceXAI's pricing page also lists SuperGrok Lite and SuperGrok Heavy but doesn't show their prices.

**Bottom line:** ChatGPT is cheaper to start paying for; Grok puts more into its first paid plan.

## What each plan adds

**ChatGPT:** Go gives you 10x Free's messages, file uploads and image creation. Plus adds voice, image generation, file analysis, deep research and Codex. Pro adds OpenAI's most capable Pro model, the dots agent and early access to new tools.

**Grok:** Free already includes real-time web and X search, voice mode and connectors. SuperGrok adds Grok 4.6, higher limits, Expert, image and video generation and Grok Bot. SuperGrok Plus adds 1080p video, much higher usage and priority access at peak times.

## Agents

Each company now sells a personal agent on top of its chatbot. [Grok Bot](/agents/compare/muse-vs-dots-vs-grok-bot) comes with SuperGrok, so the cheapest way in is $30 a month. [ChatGPT dots](/agents/pricing/chatgpt-dots) need Pro or Business Premium, so the cheapest way in is $100 a month.

## Privacy

Both use your chats to improve their models unless you opt out. On ChatGPT, turn it off in Data Controls. On grok.com, go to **Settings → Data → Improve the Model**, or use **Private Chat**, which is never used for training. On X, the setting is under Privacy & Safety.
