## What you get on each plan

- **Free:** Muse with a usage limit that refreshes. Meta doesn't publish the limit.
- **Power, $20/mo:** 500M Muse tokens a week.
- **Max, $100/mo:** 3B Muse tokens a week.

Meta describes the plans only by weekly usage. It doesn't list features, models or connectors that are limited to a paid plan.

Meta also hasn't said how many tokens a typical task uses, so there's no official way yet to turn a weekly allowance into a number of tasks. We'll add it here if Meta publishes it.

## Which plan to pick

Start on the free tier. Meta describes the paid plans only by how much usage they include, not by extra features. Upgrade to Power if you keep hitting the free limit before it refreshes, and move to Max only if Power's weekly allowance runs out too.

## Billing

- Plans **renew monthly** on the date you subscribed, until you cancel. Meta doesn't describe an annual option.
- You can **cancel any time**.
- You sign in with a **Meta account** (Facebook, Instagram, or email or phone) and agree to Meta's Subscription Terms of Service.
- You need to be **18 or older**, or the age of majority where you live.
- Muse and its subscriptions are **in limited testing** and aren't available everywhere. Muse is rolling out in the US first.

## How Muse pricing compares

Of the big-company personal agents, Muse is the only one with a free tier. [ChatGPT dots](/agents/pricing/chatgpt-dots) start at $100 a month with ChatGPT Pro. Gemini Spark needs Google AI Pro ($19.99) or higher. Grok Bot needs SuperGrok ($30) or a Cursor plan. Muse Power at $20 is in the same range as Google AI Pro and Poke Pro ($19). For a feature-by-feature view, see [Meta Muse vs ChatGPT Dots](/agents/compare/muse-vs-dots).
