## What's included

- **Your first dot**, at no extra cost.
- **An allowance for deeper work**, with extended limits in your first month.
- **Extra dots:** "in the future," price not announced.

## Billing

ChatGPT Pro is **monthly only**; OpenAI doesn't offer annual billing for it.

All three Pro tiers are **open to new subscribers**. Pro $200 includes more usage than Pro $100, but new Pro $200 subscriptions get a lower allowance than the tier used to include. If your Pro $200 subscription qualifies for grandfathering, you keep your previous allowance through **October 29, 2026**, then move to the new allowance at the same $200 price.
