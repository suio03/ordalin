## What we tested

We installed cinetic 1.5.1 (commit a6a6a02) into an empty project and gave Claude Code on Claude Opus 5.5 the brief above, headless, with no follow-up. The machine had Node, ffmpeg and uv but only Python 3.10 and no earshot, so the agent had to do the skill's setup first. We asked it to keep everything inside the project folder.

## What we found

- **It delivered a finished film.** The result was a 1920×1080, 30 fps MP4 of 13.97 seconds for "Notara 2.0", an invented team notes app, with three changes, a licensed Coverr music track, eleven Mixkit sound effects, a mix at −14 LUFS, posters and a credits file. It looks like a real product update video.
- **Setup worked without help.** The agent created a Python 3.11 environment with uv, built earshot 0.4.1 from its tagged source, and pointed the skill's caches into the project. One slip: some cinetic scripts ignore that setting and wrote three files to `~/.cinetic`. The agent noticed, moved them into the project and removed the folder.
- **The template sets the length.** The update template fits the film to the music's bars and locks its timing limits, so the ending landed at 13.97 seconds instead of 15. The next bar would have made it about 15.7 seconds. Changing the copy didn't move it.
- **The checks are strict, and some still failed.** Four visual checks flagged moments inside the template's own scenes: two camera push-and-back moves, an abrupt stop before the logo, and half a headline popping in within two frames. The agent judged them part of the template and left them. It did act on its own review once: the typed question in change 1 was hard to read, so it swapped in a large-type scene.
- **Most of the look comes from the skill.** The headline cards, notification stack, flying page and logo pull-back are cinetic's scenes. The agent wrote the brand, the copy and the content, and chose the scenes. That is why a short run already looks finished, and also why films can share a family look.

The author's demos above are longer and more varied than our 14-second run. For a real launch, budget the skill's own one to two hours, supply your actual logo and screenshots, and expect to accept the length the music gives you or tune the template.
