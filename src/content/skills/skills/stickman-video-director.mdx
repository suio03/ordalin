## What we tested

We installed the skill (commit 8643650) into an empty project and gave Claude Code on Opus 5.5 one topic in two separate headless runs: why we keep opening the fridge when we're bored. The first used Director mode in the A3 Prehistoric Cave style at 30 seconds; we approved the treatment with one follow-up message. The second used Autopilot mode in B1 Minimal Line Art. The machine had Node and FFmpeg but no image-generation tool and no text-to-speech key, and we told the agent not to sign up for anything, choose a voice or use the computer's built-in speech.

## What we found

- **Director mode delivered in under five minutes.** The treatment, The Maybe Rock, turns the topic into a caveman who keeps rolling the stone off his food store, with a deadpan payoff where the elder who stops him does the same thing. It came with cast cards, a fixed cave layout, timed narration (61 words for 30 seconds), music and sound cues and continuity joins. After approval it wrote three standalone prompts for Gemini Omni Flash. We didn't generate the clips, so we can't say how well a video model follows them.
- **Autopilot mode rendered real video, but not a finished one.** The skill's doctor check passed rendering (Node, FFmpeg, canvas, fonts) and reported no image or voice service. The agent wrote a nine-line script, drew the B1 illustrations itself in code, synthesized its own sound effects, and rendered a 20-second 1280×720 preview with captions that passed the skill's checks. It fixed two drawing problems after looking at its own frames.
- **No voice means no complete preview.** The renderer needs a narration track, so the agent used a silent placeholder, timed captions to an estimated reading pace and kept the effects quiet for a voice later. It said all of this plainly and didn't record the sample as approved.
- **Code-drawn art is simpler than the demos.** Our preview is clean but plain next to the author's B1 films, which were made with an image model. Give the agent an image tool for results closer to the gallery above.
- **It stayed in its folder.** Both runs left nothing new in the home folder beyond the usual temporary files.

For a faceless channel, Director mode is the low-cost way in if you already use a video generator. Autopilot mode is the fuller pipeline, and it's honest about what it can't do without the services you choose.
