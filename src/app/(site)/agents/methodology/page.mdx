# How we check AI agent facts

**Facts come from the maker.** Prices, plans, platforms, availability, safety features and data policies come from each company's own pages: product announcements, pricing pages and help centers. Every page links the sources it uses.

**"Not stated" means not stated.** If a maker's pages don't say something, we show "Not stated" instead of guessing or copying a number from a blog. It doesn't mean the feature is missing.

**Third-party claims stay out of the facts.** News coverage, reviews and posts sometimes report details the maker hasn't confirmed. We keep those in a separate list and don't show them as facts. When we add community reports, they'll sit in their own section, clearly marked as not verified by us.

**Every page is dated.** "Checked" shows the date we last compared the page against the sources. We re-check prices, plans and availability every week, because they change often for new agents.

**Verdicts are ours.** The "Quick verdict" and "Pick X if" lists are our reading of the facts on the page. They're opinion, and we write them only from facts shown on that page.

**No payments for placement.** No company pays to appear here or to change its ranking. If we ever add affiliate links, we'll mark them on the page and on this one.
