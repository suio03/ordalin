MVP · Light theme only
01

Editorial Index

A contemporary reference publication where judgment is visible in pacing, type, and labels.

SignatureNumbered register and fine rules turn browsing into an authored index.
Reference mixMonocle hierarchy + Are.na restraint + Product Hunt daily inventory.
StrengthClosest fit with Editorial Cut and durable trust.
RiskCan feel serious for users expecting playful AI discovery.
02

Signal Desk

A precise discovery instrument where every control, status, and row earns its place.

SignatureCompact signal strip and table-like feed keep catalogue state visible.
Reference mixLinear discipline + AlternativeTo filters + G2 taxonomy.
StrengthFastest scanning and clearest deterministic behavior.
RiskMay feel like an internal database to casual visitors.
03

Open Atlas

An approachable map of tool territories with memorable, restrained category color.

SignatureCategory territories carry color into filters and inventory blocks.
Reference mixRaindrop collections + Futurepedia labels + AlternativeTo browseability.
StrengthMost welcoming and memorable for category-led newcomers.
RiskColor can weaken authority if it expands without discipline.
04

Quiet Radar

Calm orientation through low-noise spatial cues and quietly surfaced editorial signals.

SignatureSubtle coordinate fields link search, active category, and provenance.
Reference mixCosmos provenance + Are.na intentionality + Linear restraint.
StrengthMost distinctive expression of “Navigation Radar” without a literal radar.
RiskAtmospheric boundaries require disciplined contrast.

Decision recorded: 04 · Quiet Radar.

Quiet Radar was selected and approved on 23 August 2026. This comparison remains decision evidence; implementation follows the refined homepage and the design-system files in docs/.
