# Ordalin main-theme exploration

Status: completed. **04 · Quiet Radar** was selected and approved as the Ordalin main theme on 23 August 2026.

Open `comparison.html` only to review the four-direction decision evidence. The previews use a 1440 px product-home reference surface and exactly the same content, controls, and data.

## Files

- `comparison.html` — interactive four-direction comparison and full-size views.
- `research.md` — ten-product evidence audit and frozen homepage skeleton.
- `directions.md` — complete thesis, system, strength, risk, and refusal for each direction.

## Final decision

**Quiet Radar** is the only active implementation direction. Its approved responsive reference is `round-02/quiet-radar.html`. The durable design specification is `../docs/design-system.md`; semantic tokens live in `../docs/tokens.json` and `../docs/design-tokens.css`.

Editorial Index, Signal Desk, and Open Atlas remain historical comparison material and must not be mixed into implementation without a new design decision.

## Prototype-data note

The example products are real, and their short purposes and current availability were checked against official product pages on 22 August 2026. The displayed Editor Picks, collections, tool counts, and Ordalin publication status are clearly labelled interface samples and are not launch claims.
