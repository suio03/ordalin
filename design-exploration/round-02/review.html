02 / Refinement

What became more precise

The atmosphere remains quiet, but usability no longer depends on low contrast or decorative transparency.

01

Search without a gate

The search field anchors the page while twelve real example tools remain browseable without a query.

02

Visible truth signals

Category, pricing, and check date stay subordinate but readable; invented ratings and engagement are absent.

03

Solid quiet layers

Depth comes from color, border, and spacing. The interface does not rely on blur or continuous repainting.

04

Mobile reordering

Collections follow the feed, then Editor Picks and methodology—matching the approved MVP mobile hierarchy.

03 / Primary interaction

Search and narrow

The primary interaction has five explicit states. Feedback begins immediately; no network or animation is required to understand the result.

01Ready

All twelve examples visible; Today selected; result count announced.

02Focused

Strong blue focus ring; `/` moves keyboard focus directly to search.

03Narrowed

Typing, category, and pricing update rows and result count immediately.

04Empty

Explicit “No tools found” message suggests changing term or filter.

05Reset

Reset restores All and returns focus to search; Escape clears a focused query.
