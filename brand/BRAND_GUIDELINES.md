# Ordalin logo guidelines

Final direction: **Editorial Cut**, restored from Round 01 without shape changes.

## Core idea

The heavy O establishes stability and inventory. Its white counter keeps the mark open, while the upper-right editorial cut represents selection: removing noise to reveal a useful path.

## Assets

- `assets/svg/ordalin-logo-primary.svg` — full charcoal lockup for light backgrounds.
- `assets/svg/ordalin-logo-reversed.svg` — full white lockup for dark backgrounds.
- `assets/svg/ordalin-symbol-primary.svg` — standalone charcoal symbol.
- `assets/svg/ordalin-symbol-reversed.svg` — standalone white symbol.
- `assets/svg/ordalin-avatar-dark.svg` — social/avatar application.
- `assets/favicon/` — SVG and PNG favicon exports.
- `assets/png/` — transparent PNG lockup and symbol exports.

## Color

- Charcoal: `#111111`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Keep the core logo monochrome. Product accent colors may appear around it, but not inside the symbol.

## Minimum size

- Standalone symbol: 16 px digital minimum.
- Full lockup: 120 px wide digital minimum.

At 16 px, use the standalone symbol only. Do not use the full lockup.

## Clear space

Maintain clear space equal to half the symbol's inner counter width on all sides. Do not place text, card edges, or other icons inside this area.

## Usage

- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, or add gradients.
- Do not place the symbol inside an additional circle.
- Do not retype the wordmark. The production SVG contains outlined letterforms and has no font dependency.
- On busy imagery, use the dark avatar container or place the logo on a calm solid field.

## Status

The assets have passed SVG syntax and local raster-render checks. Visual collision screening is not formal trademark clearance.
